# Benchmark data attribution

Epoch AI, “Capabilities & benchmarking”, https://epoch.ai/benchmarks

Public dataset: https://epoch.ai/data/benchmark_data.zip

Epoch AI's data is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Quota retains the published ECI point estimates and intervals. Derived scenario
indices use the dataset's benchmark calibration parameters, source metadata and
configuration-specific observations. Quota applies its own scenario selection,
duplicate/conflict exclusions, complete-configuration selection, finite-fit rules,
sensitivity analysis and presentation bands. These derived indices are not
official Epoch AI domain ECI results or an endorsement by Epoch AI.

Raw Artificial Analysis, LiveBench and vendor report references remain attributed
to their respective publishers in the application and dataset provenance fields.
